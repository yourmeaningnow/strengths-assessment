What to do next

Now take this into your
splay and chain.

Your labour profile is the first layer of data for your splay and chain exercise. Here is how to use it:

  1. 1 Note your top two or three labour types from your profile above. These are the kinds of work that genuinely energise you.
  2. 2 Open your splay — the prompted brainstorm in the course. Let your top types guide you as you write. What roles, projects, or fields involve a lot of this kind of work?
  3. 3 Use the job areas above as sparks, not constraints. Add anything they remind you of. The point is to get everything on paper without filtering.
  4. 4 Once you have your splay down, build your chain — order the items from least to most meaningful to try. Your labour profile can help you sense-check the order.

Remember: this is data, not destiny. Finding your meaning is iterative. This quiz has given you a better starting point — the rest is walking the road.
